Meet Jerron.

Owner & Designer at Ralph Media & Design.

Built from a love for design.

My name is Jerron Ralph, and I'm the owner and designer behind Ralph Media & Design. I'm 15 years old, and I've always enjoyed creating things, especially when I can turn an idea into something that actually looks good.

I have a strong attention to detail, and that pushes me to keep improving until the graphics, layout and final product are something I'm proud to put my name on.

I also run a property service business called Ralph Property Services. Running another business has helped me become a better leader and entrepreneur, while teaching me the importance of showing up, communicating and getting things done right.

Ralph Media & Design is where I get to combine that business mindset with creativity.

The Goal

Make your brand look like you mean business.

Whether you need a graphic, a website, a promotional video or digital content, the goal is simple: create something you're proud to put in front of your customers.
